TakeMeter — College Discussion Label Definitions

Community: r/college
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/college/

Each Reddit post receives exactly one label based on its primary topic or purpose.

1. academic_advice

Posts primarily requesting guidance or recommendations about academic matters, including studying, courses, grades, exams, professors, course registration, and degree requirements.

Examples:
- A student asking whether to withdraw from a difficult course.
- A student requesting advice about preparing for final exams.

Boundary rule:
If the post is primarily about coursework or academic decisions and seeks guidance, label it academic_advice. If it mainly shares a personal experience without requesting advice, use college_experience.


2. college_experience

Posts primarily sharing personal experiences, accomplishments, frustrations, or reflections about attending college.

Examples:
- A student celebrating making the dean's list.
- A student describing their first semester of college.

Boundary rule:
Use college_experience when the main purpose is sharing a personal story, rather than discussing a specific campus-life or financial topic.


3. campus_life

Posts primarily discussing college social life, roommates, dormitories, friendships, student organizations, campus facilities, or extracurricular activities.

Examples:
- A student discussing difficulties with a roommate.
- A student describing their experience joining a campus club.

Boundary rule:
If the central subject is a roommate, housing, friendship, or campus activity, use campus_life even if the writer describes a personal experience.


4. financial_concerns

Posts primarily discussing tuition, scholarships, financial aid, student loans, college expenses, or paying for education.

Examples:
- A student discussing difficulty paying tuition.
- A student asking about changes to a financial aid package.

Boundary rule:
Use financial_concerns whenever the central issue is paying for college or managing educational expenses, even if the post asks for advice.


GENERAL LABELING RULES

1. Read the full post, not just its title.
2. Assign exactly one of the four labels.
3. Classify by the primary topic and purpose.
4. Prioritize financial_concerns for financial questions.
5. Prioritize campus_life for roommate, housing, and social-life discussions.
6. Use academic_advice for academic guidance requests.
7. Use college_experience for primarily personal reflections and achievements.
8. Record ambiguous labeling decisions in the dataset's note column.
9. Do not change labels merely to improve dataset balance.
10. Remove duplicate posts before training.

All sample examples above are illustrative, not collected Reddit posts.
